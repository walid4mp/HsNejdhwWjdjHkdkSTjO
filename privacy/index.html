WHSTO.com is an informational website. We use no tracking cookies, ads or analytics tools. The site stores only your language preference in your browser (localStorage) to remember your choice. Hosting servers may record standard technical data (IP address, browser type) for security and operation.

If you email or message us on WhatsApp, we use your details only to reply.
